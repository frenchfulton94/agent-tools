- validPartyCodeAlphabet has 20 characters, 23456789CFGHJMPQRVWX.
- isValidPartyCode accepts 2..6 characters per half, both halves equal length,

  CFGHJMPQ false).
- Pure-digit codes may use 0 and 1 (0123-4567 true).
- isValidPartyCode rejects lowercase (cfgh-jmpq false), although
  start(definition:partyCode:) documents that it uppercases the code. Uppercase
