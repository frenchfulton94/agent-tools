How the URL was found
1. https://developer.apple.com/metal/cpp/ (HTTP 200, 108 KB) no longer links an archive.
   No ".zip" appears in its HTML, and none of its scripts builds one. The installation
   steps point at https://github.com/apple/metal-cpp ("Access source code").
2. The conventional path https://developer.apple.com/metal/cpp/files/metal-cpp_<version>.zip
   still serves archives. HEAD probes, 2026-10-18:
     200 application/zip   26, 26.4
     300 text/html         26.1, 26.2, 26.3, 26.4.1, 26.5 through 26.10
     404                   14.2, 15, 15.2, 27, 27.0, 27.1, 27.2, 28
3. 26.4 is the newest version that returns an archive. The Metal 4 headers confirm it.

fetch_metal_cpp.sh pins the sha256 above and refuses an archive that differs.
The download lands in the gitignored pipeline/tmp/metal-cpp/. Nothing from the archive
is committed.
