regression ... and a wording change could plausibly address it." For

  now-reverted Task 9 description edit, attributing it to "session-to-
  session routing variance under `--allowedTools`... not the edit

  which corroborates that it is edit-independent.

## Decision: REVERT

Applying Step 6's rule mechanically:

- **KEEP** requires no confirmed regression AND at least 1 of 4 Duo FIRE
  rows holding more than baseline. The Duo condition is satisfied (4 of 4
  hold vs. 0 of 4 at baseline), but there **is** a confirmed regression
  (`ad-fire-5`). **KEEP fails.**
- The revision round was available but not plausible (see above), so it
  was not used.
- **REVERT applies**: "REVERT both description edits if the KEEP
  condition fails after the revision round."

Both `SKILL.md` descriptions were reverted to their exact pre-Task-10
text; `git diff` confirms zero diff on both files. The `triggers.md`
changes (Step 3) are kept regardless, per the brief: the `ad-fire-9`
reword fixes a corpus defect independent of the description, and the new
`app-release` row documents the boundary either way.

This is a mechanical, rule-driven outcome, not a judgment call: despite
strong evidence that `ad-fire-5`'s instability is pre-existing harness
noise (the `--allowedTools` leak, item 6) rather than something caused by
this task's wording, the brief's rule does not carve out an exception for
a "plausibly unrelated" flip (the same stance Task 6 took for this exact
prompt). The Duo routing fix itself worked — all four Duo rows held,
which the single-run Task 9 attempt never achieved — but it cannot ship
this phase under the rule as written.
