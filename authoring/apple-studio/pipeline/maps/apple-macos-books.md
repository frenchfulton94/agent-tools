# Chapter map: apple-macos books

Targets (Tasks 2–3): `mac-app-structure.md`, `mac-windows-menus-commands.md`,
`appkit-interop.md`, `mac-sandbox-and-files.md`.

Book scope per brief (2026-08-04-phase4-macos/task-1-brief.md): this is a single
~2022 book (macOS by Tutorials v1.0.0) built around three sample apps (a
window-based SwiftUI app, an AppKit menu-bar app, and a SwiftUI document-based
app) plus a terminal-automation app. It is judgment-only source material —
mapped ruthlessly. Walkthrough chapters (data-model plumbing, Terminal/`sips`
command wrapping, Services/Shortcuts automation, timers/alerts/notifications)
carry no durable native-Mac-design judgment and are skipped even where they're
otherwise well-written. Chapters 15–16 (Mac App Store submission, notarization,
external distribution) are `app-release` territory per the brief and are
skipped entirely regardless of content quality. Every range start below was
verified with `sed -n '<start>p'` against the converted corpus file.
