# Mission established: teammates first, author second

The learner chose "both, teammates first" over an author-only or teammate-only framing:
lessons assume no OpenSpec knowledge, and author-level detail goes in clearly marked
appendices. They also chose full coverage — all thirteen lessons, all twelve schemas
driven end to end — over a ten- or eight-lesson trim.

**Implications:** every lesson must stand alone for a newcomer, so a lesson cannot
depend on an appendix. Vocabulary gets defined before it is used, which is why
[[0002-glossary-is-load-bearing]] matters more here than it would in an author-only
package. Future lessons keep the appendix convention rather than inlining depth.
